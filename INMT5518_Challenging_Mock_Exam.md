# SECTION 3: IN-DEPTH BILINGUAL EXPLANATIONS & TRAP ANALYSES

### Question 1
- **Correct Answer**: **C**
- **Analytical Rationale**:  
  Dr. Eliyahu Goldratt’s foundational definition of Supply Chain Management explicitly defines **Throughput** as: *"the rate at which the system generates money through sales"* (Textbook Ch. 1). Goods that are fabricated and sitting unsold in warehouses do NOT constitute throughput; inventory only generates throughput when an actual monetary sale to the final customer is consummated.
- **【考点释义 / 核心精解】**:  
  教材第 1 章高德拉特核心哲学原话考查：**有效产出（Throughput）是指整个系统通过实现终端销售而创造现金的速率**。没有完成最终销售并转化为货款的库存，只能算作占压资金的库存（Inventory），绝不能计入有效产出！
- **【易错陷阱剖析】**:  
  陷阱 A 误将生产能力（Capacity）当作 Throughput；陷阱 B 误将产成品库存（Finished Goods）当作 Throughput。请牢记：**未售出的货物堆在仓库中不是 Throughput，卖成钱才算！**

### Question 2
- **Correct Answer**: **B**
- **Analytical Rationale**:  
  Henry Ford's River Rouge plant epitomized extreme **Vertical Integration** (owning the entire supply chain from raw iron ore mines to glass mills and assembly tracks). While this achieved unprecedented scale economies for a single standardized product (the black Model T), it created catastrophic structural rigidity. When consumer markets matured in the late 1920s and 1930s and demanded varied styling, colors, and comfort features, Ford’s massive dedicated machines could not retool quickly, resulting in devastating market share loss to more agile competitors like General Motors. Modern supply chains shifted to **Virtual Integration**, focusing on core competencies and partnering with specialized experts.
- **【考点释义 / 核心精解】**:  
  福特胭脂河工厂是**纵向一体化（Vertical Integration）**的工业巅峰（81 小时铁矿石变汽车）。但其衰退的本质是：**重资产纵向一体化带来了极端的战略刚性**。当市场由粗放式短缺经济转向追求多元化、个性化、款式频繁更迭的买方市场时，福特完全无法灵活应对，导致其市场份额从 50% 暴跌至 20% 以下。现代供应链因而转向**虚拟整合（Virtual Integration）**，各企业聚焦于自身的核心竞争力（Core Competencies）。
- **【易错陷阱剖析】**:  
  陷阱 A、C、D 均属于编造的伪常识。记住：导致福特失败的是**缺乏应对市场多样化需求的敏捷性与柔性**（*"They can have any color they want as long as it's black"*）。

- **Correct Answer**: **D**
- **Analytical Rationale**:

- **【考点释义 / 核心精解】**:

### Question 19
